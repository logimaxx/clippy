What we build

Webklip is an online clipboard for sharing text and files over a private link — a temporary workspace that syncs live and expires by default. No account is required to try it. Clips expire by default after 15 minutes, and expired content is deleted — we do not keep abandoned clips “just in case.”

That ephemerality is the product. Chat apps, cloud drives, and classic pastebins keep permanent copies; Webklip is for the moment you need to move something between devices, then let it go.

Accounts and teams

The everyday flow stays anonymous. When you want more than a throwaway link, a free account adds a dashboard of the clips you own, teams you can invite people to by email, and API keys for the REST API and CLI. {{OAUTH_SIGN_IN}}

Team clips live at webklip.com/team/clip-name, so a name you use every week is a URL you can type from memory. Members are admins, members, or read-only viewers, and team clips can be restricted to the team. You can delete a team, or your whole account and everything in it, yourself — no support ticket.

Who we are

Webklip is a product of LogiMaxx Systems, based in Romania. We operate the public service at webklip.com and care about keeping the trust model simple and verifiable.

How trust works here

  • No account required — paste, share the link, done; accounts are opt-in
  • Short default lifetime — 15 minutes unless you extend it
  • Burn after read — optional one-view deletion, with a 7-day safety cap if never opened
  • Passphrase end-to-end encryption — optional zero-knowledge protect mode for sensitive content
  • Link-as-secret — anyone with the URL can access unprotected clips; we say so plainly
