What we build

Webklip is a temporary workspace for sharing text and files over a private link. No account is required to try it. Clips expire by default after 15 minutes, and expired content is deleted — we do not keep abandoned clips “just in case.”

That ephemerality is the product. Chat apps and cloud drives keep permanent copies; Webklip is for the moment you need to move something between devices, then let it go.

Who we are

Webklip is a product of LogiMaxx Systems, based in Romania. We operate the public service at webklip.com and care about keeping the trust model simple and verifiable.

How trust works here

  • No account required — paste, share the link, done
  • Short default lifetime — 15 minutes unless you extend it
  • Burn after read — optional one-view deletion, with a 7-day safety cap if never opened
  • Passphrase end-to-end encryption — optional zero-knowledge protect mode for sensitive content
  • Link-as-secret — anyone with the URL can access unprotected clips; we say so plainly
