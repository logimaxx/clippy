What is Webklip?

Webklip is a free temporary workspace for moving text and files between devices. Create a private link, paste your content, and open it on any browser — phone, tablet, or desktop.

Real-time sync, optional PIN locks, end-to-end encryption, and automatic expiry make it ideal for secure sharing that does not linger in chat history or email inboxes. Share instantly, then let it disappear.
